WAKE

Your rowing machine already measures everything. WAKE reads the WaterRower S4 monitor over USB and turns it into analog instruments that behave like water, 17 games, and a flight down the California coast on a real 3D globe. It runs on the tablet you already have.

The instruments

Speed, power and stroke rate on real dials, a water paddle that spins at the measured speed, and a live trace of boat speed over power. The thing that matters is the decay between strokes — rowers call it the run of the boat, and it is the whole reason to take the next stroke. Most apps hold the last reading flat. This one models the paddle slowing down: quadratic drag, ω(t) = ω₀/(1 + (k/I)ω₀t).

The games

Pace boats, ghost races against your own best, a zombie that gains on you when you ease off, a side-scrolling runner, boss fights, a canyon chase, an isometric city that builds itself one block per stroke. Every one shows the same vitals strip so you can read your numbers while you play.

Coast Flight

A CesiumJS globe with satellite imagery and real terrain. Power above 60 W is lift, boat speed is airspeed, and the route flies itself down the California coast. Your progress persists, so the 900 km is a trip across many sessions.

Install

  1. Open this page in the browser on your rowing tablet and tap Download APK.
  2. Allow the install when Android asks. Nothing is replaced and nothing needs root.
  3. Plug the S4 monitor into the tablet's USB port and open WAKE. Grant USB access.
  4. It connects on its own and the gauges come alive. Take a stroke.

The laptop dashboard is optional — clone the repo and run node server/server.js for live capture, an event stream and protocol decoding. The app finds it on your Wi-Fi by itself.

Built to be safe on a leased machine

  • No root. No system modification. No changes to boot behaviour.
  • Ergatta is untouched and remains the fallback — WAKE is just another app you launch.
  • Nothing leaves your network except map tiles for the flight screen.
  • No account, no telemetry, no ads. Records live in the tablet's own storage.

Hardware

Built against a WaterRower in an Ergatta fit-out, Android 9 tablet, S4 monitor 04D8:000A over CDC-ACM. Any WaterRower S4 that exposes USB serial should work; other Android tablets should too. reports welcome
